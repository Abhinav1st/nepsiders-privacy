Nepsiders Badminton — Privacy Policy

Effective: 23 October 2026

Nepsiders Badminton helps club participants request Monday and Tuesday badminton sessions, view rosters, record whether a club payment has been made, and communicate suggestions.

Information you provide

When you make a booking, you may provide your name, email address, requested session, venue preference, booking status, selected amount ($20 or $40), and whether payment was declared or verified. Suggestions you submit may also be processed. The clubhouse may require an account with its hosting or sign-in provider to access certain functions.

How information is used

Information is used to organise badminton sessions, manage attendance and confirmation, verify payments made outside the app, contact participants about bookings, and respond to suggestions. The app does not process card payments. We do not use booking contact information for advertising.

Where information is processed

The iOS app opens the Nepsiders hosted clubhouse in an embedded browser. Information entered there may be processed and stored by the clubhouse hosting and authentication services. If Firebase is enabled for bookings, Google Firebase may also process booking records. Browser sessions and required service logs may be retained by those providers. Details of the exact providers and retention settings will be updated as the booking service is configured.

Sharing and security

Booking records are intended for authorised club organisers and technical service providers operating the app. We do not sell player information. Access controls and service-provider security protections are used to reduce unauthorised access, although no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and your choices

Records are kept only as long as reasonably needed to organise club activities, handle enquiries, resolve disputes and meet applicable obligations. You can ask the organiser to access, correct or delete your booking information. Requests can be made through the clubhouse's suggestion/contact channel. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Updates

We may update this policy as the booking and sign-in services change. Changes will be posted on this page.

For privacy requests, use the contact or suggestion channel in the Nepsiders clubhouse.
