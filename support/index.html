Nepsiders Badminton — Support

Need help with Nepsiders Badminton? Contact the club organiser for booking questions, payment confirmation, app issues or privacy requests.

Contact support

Email: abhinav_sarmah@hotmail.com

Please include a short description of your issue, your session date if relevant, and your device model and iOS version for technical problems. Do not send passwords, bank account details or card information.

Bookings and payment confirmation

Unpaid bookings remain tentative. Payments are made outside the app, and the organiser verifies payment before confirming your spot. If your payment status looks incorrect, email support with your name and session date.

App troubleshooting

If the clubhouse does not load, check your internet connection and try the reload button. If the issue continues, contact support and describe what you see.

Privacy requests

You can email support to request access, correction or deletion of your booking information.
